Murrumbidgee River Pulse

Every reach, every day · modelled 2015–2034
The ground is the terrain of each subcatchment (hillshaded). The river stands just proud of it, rising with that day’s routed discharge at each reach. The relief slider scales the terrain and the river together. A dark cloud floats high above the middle of each subcatchment that has had at least 40 mm of rain in 60 days, bigger and darker the wetter it has been. The ground is tinted by rolling 30-day potential evaporation. NARCliM2.0 SSP2-4.5 (ACCESS-ESM1-5) driving an uncalibrated GR4J and Muskingum model — indicative only.
