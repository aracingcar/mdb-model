Rainfall-runoff areas

Click an area or its dot to select it.

Each dot is where one node’s runoff joins the network, and its area sets that node’s share of the basin’s runoff. Rain and evaporation are still averaged over the whole basin, so moving a border changes how the basin’s runoff is divided, not how much rain falls. Basin borders (heavy lines) are fixed. Edits are saved in this browser; apply them to update flows. Moving or deleting a river node’s dot edits the routing network too, as in the routing editor: its reaches become straight lines, and a deleted node’s flow and runoff go to the node below it.
Routing network

Select a node to edit.

Edits are saved in this browser. Apply to update flows. Add flow split puts a split node on a channel; its diversion can end at a node, or on a channel or another diversion. A flow split sends the chosen share of a node’s flow to another node (or out of the river system) and the rest continues downstream. A network exit sends a basin’s flow into another river system, and sits exactly on the entry node it feeds. New tributaries take the chosen share of the selected node’s catchment runoff; total catchment area is preserved. System links, dams, diversions and calibration nodes are protected; deleting a basin outlet moves its basin’s flow reporting to the node below.
